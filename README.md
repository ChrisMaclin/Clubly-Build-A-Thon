# Clubly-Build-A-Thon
Clubly is a platform designed to help students discover, explore, and connect with college clubs.

Developed by Team Cheeseburger.

About

Clubly aims to bring college clubs together in one place, making it easier for students to discover communities, explore interests, and get involved in campus activities.

The project is currently under development.

Club discovery and exploration

Student-focused platform

Designed to connect students with communities
